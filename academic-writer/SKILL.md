# Academic Writing Workflow
1. **Structural Integrity:** Enforce standard academic topology (Abstract, Introduction, Methodology/Background, Empirical Analysis, Conclusion).
2. **Tone Enforcement:** Maintain a rigorous, objective, and analytical tone. Strip all rhetorical questions, conversational transitions, and promotional adjectives.
3. **Domain Adaptation:** 
   - For **technical papers**, isolate mechanical constraints, system architecture, and quantitative benchmarking.
   - For **political/social papers**, analyze competing paradigms and systemic incentives. Maintain strict neutrality and evaluate claims based strictly on empirical evidence or historical precedent. Do not moralize.
4. **Argumentation:** Identify logical fallacies or unsupported assertions in the provided text. Demand specific evidence to bridge logical gaps.
